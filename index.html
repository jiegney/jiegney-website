About Me

Building digital experiences that move the needle

I'm a Digital Channels & Customer Experience leader with 15+ years across banking, NBFC lending, broking, crypto and FinTech. I currently lead Digital Channels & Innovations at HDB Financial Services Limited — an NBFC and HDFC Bank subsidiary — overseeing the HDB OnTheGo mobile app, HDBFS website, WhatsApp journeys, Chatbot (Ask Priya) and CleverTap.

My work sits at the intersection of product management, growth marketing and data — building journeys that move customers from discovery to application to loyalty. I hold a PMP® certification, an MSc in Banking & Finance from Middlesex University London, and an Advanced Certification in Digital Marketing & MarTech from IIT Roorkee.

I lead a team of 8 direct reports spanning product management, data analytics, campaign management and product marketing, all reporting to the Chief Digital & Marketing Officer.
